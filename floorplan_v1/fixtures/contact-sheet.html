PH floor-plan fixture library

55 symbols, drawn in metres. Each card shows the symbol large, then again at 42 px/m — the scale your plans actually render at — because that second view is the one that tells you whether a symbol survives. The dashed red outline, where it appears, is the declared footprint: art outside it (dining chairs) sits in the clearance zone by design.

Placement convention

  • Local space is metres. The wall a fixture backs onto is at y = 0 — the top of the picture.
  • It faces +y, into the room. Origin is the back-left corner of the footprint.
  • Rotation is orthogonal only: N = 0°, E = 90°, S = 180°, W = 270°.
  • Place with one transform: translate(px,py) scale(S,−S) rotate(θ) — it carries the metre→pixel conversion and the y-flip together.
  • Keep vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke" or every line multiplies by S.
